VorxEngine is a lightweight 2D game engine for those who actually want to make games instead of fighting with bloated interfaces. We took the simplicity of Python, squeezed maximum speed out of it with Cython, and strapped on a rock-solid SDL2 renderer. No garbage, no overhead — just pure performance and fully open-source code
